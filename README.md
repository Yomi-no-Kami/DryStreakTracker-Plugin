# Dry Streak Tracker

Dry Streak Tracker is a RuneLite plugin that tracks how dry you are between unique drops at supported bosses, raids, and other encounters.

## Features

- Automatically tracks your dry streak between unique drops.
- Side panel showing your current dry streak, longest dry streak, and kills tracked for each encounter.
- Tracks your unique drops and how many of each you've received.
- Shows the killcount of your last unique drop.
- Highlights when you're currently on your longest dry streak.
- Notifications for unique drops, pets, and new dry streak records.
- Supports bosses, raids, and other loot-based encounters.
- Tracking is saved separately for each RuneScape account.
- Individual encounters can be reset at any time.

## How It Works

Each kill without a tracked unique increases your dry streak.

When you receive a unique, your current dry streak ends and the plugin records the drop before starting your next streak.

Your longest dry streak is saved so you can see just how unlucky you've managed to get.
